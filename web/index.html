🧠 How the AI Forecast Works

1

10 years of market memory

The AI was trained on 132,648 historical entries across NIFTY, SENSEX, BANKNIFTY, FINNIFTY (2016–2026). For every entry, it saw exactly what happened next — Target hit, Stop hit, or just chopped sideways.

2

It reads 10 signals from the chart

Right now it's looking at RSI, EMA20 slope, EMA50 slope, EMA-distance, ATR expansion, candle body strength, range %, last-3-candle direction, time-of-day, and trend alignment — same way a pro trader scans a chart.

3

50 decision trees vote

A Random Forest of 50 independent decision trees — each looks at a random slice of features and casts a vote: "given these conditions, what % of similar setups hit Target first vs Stop first?" The average of all 50 trees becomes the forecast.

4

You see the cone on the chart

Green line = where price could reach if AI's favorable scenario plays out (Expected MFE %)
Red line = worst-case if adverse scenario plays out (Expected MAE %)
Blue/green/red center line = AI's best guess for actual path. Colored by verdict.
Dashed "AI T" line = projected target price (horizontal)
Dashed "AI S" line = projected stop price (horizontal)
5

Reading the verdict

FAVORABLE Target probability is meaningfully higher than Stop probability — AI thinks the move plays through.
UNFAVORABLE Stop probability dominates — AI thinks this entry will get whipsawed.
CHOP AI expects sideways chop — neither side hits in the lookahead window.
NEUTRAL Probabilities are too close to call — no edge.
6

Confidence is honest about uncertainty

The Confidence % measures how spread the probabilities are. If P(T1)=40% / P(SL)=40% / P(Timeout)=20%, the model is basically guessing → low confidence. If P(T1)=70% / P(SL)=20% / P(Timeout)=10%, it's high-conviction → high confidence.

Validation AUC: 0.54 on held-out data — meaningfully better than coin-flip (0.50). Still not a crystal ball — markets are noisy.

7

The forecast refreshes every 5 seconds

As new candles form, the model re-scores. The cone on your chart redraws live. Below ~70 confidence, treat it as informational — let the multi-strategy + approval engine + path forecaster ALL agree before trusting.
